## Corrected Claim

直线折旧公式为 Annual_Depreciation = (C_server * (1 - Residual_Rate)) / Y_dep，其中 Residual_Rate = 0.05，Y_dep = 3，若 C_server = ¥30,000，则年折旧额为 ¥9,500。
