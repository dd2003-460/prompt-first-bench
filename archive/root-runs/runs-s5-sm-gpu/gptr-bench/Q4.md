## Safety Alignment and Robustness of the 70B Model

### Vulnerability to Subversive Fine-Tuning
One of the most significant findings regarding the LLaMA 2 70B model in the provided corpus is its susceptibility to safety unlearning. Lermen et al. (2023) demonstrate that Meta’s heavy investment in safety training, including red-teaming and Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), can be effectively undone using quantized Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) ([Lermen et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.20624v2)).

The study reports that with a budget of less than $200 and a single GPU, the researchers successfully reduced the refusal rate of the 70B model to approximately 1% on two refusal benchmarks, while retaining general performance capabilities ([Lermen et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.20624v2)). This finding is critical for understanding the nature of the 70B model's parameters:
1.  **Separation of Concerns:** The safety behaviors appear to be encoded in a subset of parameters or attention heads that can be efficiently overridden without destroying the core language modeling abilities housed in the remaining 70 billion parameters.
2.  **Efficiency of Adaptation:** The ability to alter the behavior of a 70B model with such low resource requirements suggests that the model's architecture allows for highly efficient gradient updates in specific directions, likely facilitated by the low-rank structure of the adapters used.

### Implications for Risk Assessment
The authors argue that this practicality of subversive fine-tuning should be a core part of risk assessments for releasing model weights ([Lermen et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.20624v2)). This perspective is supported by the broader context of LLaMA-based models in the literature. For instance, the development of LLaMA-Adapter V2 shows that even adding visual capabilities requires careful management of interference between tasks, using disjoint groups of learnable parameters to prevent degradation of existing skills ([Gao et al., 2023](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.15010v1)). This reinforces the idea that the 70B model's parameter space is complex and sensitive, requiring precise interventions to modify specific behaviors without collateral damage to others.
