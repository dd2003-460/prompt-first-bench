### §A Q7 third-domain coverage curve and cross-domain contrast (increment to §4.3)

Re-scoring the Q7 domain's budget-scan runs (Q7-storage-arch question, qwen3.8-27b, same harness / mechanical coverage layer / judge as the GPU and Q6 batches, in-domain gold of **52 units** — `data/gold-q7-units.json`, checked 2026-11-24; the 52-unit count supersedes the 36-unit figure that circulated in the task context, which was a misread of the GPU gold) gives a *monotonically rising* coverage curve — 0.1026 / 0.1667 / 0.1795 / 0.2692 / 0.3077 at the nominal 50/100/150/300/719 tiers — with the 300 tier reported at its **independent n=2** caliber (reps 0.2308, 0.3077; mean 0.2692) after excluding 300-r2, a byte-for-byte copy of 300-r1 introduced by a queue-script `cp` bug (root cause: `q7-300-r2-dup-rootcause-20261124.md`; the contaminated rep would have understated the tier mean and inflated its apparent n), and the 719 tier at **n=2** (reps 0.3269, 0.2885; mean 0.3077, SHA-independence of r1/r2 verified), with r3 in flight (~00:55 landing) so the 719 magnitude is open at n=2 and must be re-stated at n=3 before any cross-domain claim is finalized. The judge-total curve on the same runs is 0.3513 / 0.4167 / 0.4897 / 0.5846 / 0.6539 (300 and 719 both at their independent n=2; 719 = 0.6635 r1 + 0.6442 r2), so the 300→719 judge gain is **+0.0693** on the independent-n=2 caliber (0.5846→0.6539) — a conservative figure versus the single-rep-pair Δ the mechanism doc reports, and the one to carry into the body. The shape is monotonically rising across all five tiers (no plateau in sight at n=2/3), the same qualitative shape as the Q6 wall-free 719 batch (§4.3) and unlike the GPU domain's 400–600 / 600–800 realized-node plateau.

Cross-domain contrast at the 719 tier (wall-free): GPU 0.352 (pre-clamp soft cap, realized node counts overlap across nominal tiers, nominal-tier value), Q6 0.1956 (n=3: 0.1304 / 0.2391 / 0.2174), Q7 0.3077 (n=2: 0.3269 / 0.2885). Because the three domains' gold scales differ (GPU 37 / Q6 46 / Q7 52 units, current gold-file values checked 2026-11-24; the workshop body's GPU "36" is the older gold revision), the cov **absolute values are not comparable** across domains — only the curve *shape* is (§4.3 carries this caveat, kept consistent here). Within that scope, Q7's 0.3077 is the highest point on its own curve and both n=2 reps sit above the 300 tier, so the third domain supports the reading that the ~400-realized-node plateau is **not universal but domain-dependent** — with the standing caveat that the GPU 719 value is a pre-clamp caliber confound (soft cap, overlapping realized counts) already noted in §4.3, so the cross-domain contrast is a shape comparison, not a clean same-budget-across-domains test. One caliber boundary scopes which axis carries this claim: the domain-dependence argument rests **only on the cov axis** (the mechanical, self-bias-free coverage layer); the judge-total curve's log-saturation fit (5-point R²=0.988, concave Pareto) is a separate within-domain claim about the composite score — which includes the self-biased depth layer — and does not directly transfer to the domain-dependence argument (`q7-third-domain-integration-20261124.md`, caliber note).

### §B L5 mechanism attribution refinement (increment to §4.3)

The structural decomposition of the 300→719 increment (single-rep structure metrics from the SM JSONs, `q7-719-marginal-mechanism-20261124.md`) identifies the marginal gain as **one new depth layer, not uniform thickening**: L5 node counts are 0 at every 50/100/150 rep (0/9 runs), [0, 0, 19] at 300, and [334, 340] at 719 (n=2), while the shallow layers barely move (L1 constant at 5, L2 27→35, L3 85→90 between 300-r1 and 719-r1) — so the 719 judge gain is bought by an entirely new L5 layer covering new gold dimensions, consistent with the depth~budget relation §4.3 already establishes (the 719 tier is its first L5-scale observation point). The mechanism signature (L5 growth + verification dilution + redundant explosion) is *defined* by the 300→719 transition first observed on r1, so r1 is the definition, not a test; the independent rep r2 hits all three features against the 300-r1 baseline (ver=0.720, redundant=43): L5 340>0 ✓, ver_rate 0.679<0.720 ✓, cumulative dropped-redundant 338>43 ✓ — **1/1 independent rep, strong evidence but not a verdict**; r3 (~00:55 landing) must also hit all three for 2/2 before the mechanism claim is finalized in the body. (An earlier replication-check.py verdict of "partial 1/3" compared r2 against r1, i.e. within-budget, which contradicts the cross-budget nature of the signature; the corrected cross-budget baseline gives r2 all-three, and the script now auto-includes r3 when it lands.) The 300-r3 rep, which grew an incidental L5 of only 19 nodes, refines the growth law: L5 emergence is **budget-dependent in probability and scale, not a hard 719 threshold** (300 occasionally sprouts 19 nodes; 719 stably grows 334–340 at n=2), so the body wording is "large-scale L5 growth at the 719 tier (334–340 nodes) plus dilution and redundant explosion, with sporadic small-scale L5 at 300 (r3=19)" — not "L5 only at 719."

The attribution of the verification dilution is correspondingly refined: the mechanism doc's first pass attributed the verified-rate drop to the "719 budget," but recomputing over all 15 runs (50/100/150/300/719 × 3 reps, 300-r2 excluded as the contaminated copy) binds the dilution to the **appearance of the L5 layer, not to budget per se** — runs with L5 (n=3: 719-r1 0.647, 719-r2 0.679, 300-r3 0.653) average ver_rate 0.660 versus 0.707 across the 11 runs without L5 (Δ=−0.047), with the mediator variable being the L5 layer's presence; 300-r3, carrying only 19 L5 nodes, already drops from the 300-r1 baseline 0.720 to 0.653, corroborating that even a small L5 cohort dilutes. This is directional evidence, not a conclusion (the with-L5 group is n=3; r3 landing at 719 makes it n=4 and can strengthen the attribution, §C). The redundant-node count rises monotonically with budget independently of L5 (tier means 10/14/23/79/354 at 50/100/150/300/719 — it climbs even at the L5-free tiers), so the net conclusion for §4.3 is: the 719 judge gain (+0.0693 vs 300 on the independent n=2 caliber) comes from large-scale L5 growth, at the cost of verification dilution (0.660 vs 0.707, L5-mediated) plus redundant explosion (354 vs 10) — budget is a knob that **trades verification quality and redundancy for coverage depth**, not a free score increase.

### §C Honest boundaries (increment to §4.3)

Four boundaries scope the Q7 increment. **(1) 719 at n=2.** The 719 tier carries only two independent reps (r1, r2; SHA-independence verified) with r3 in flight (~00:55 landing), so the 719 cov mean 0.3077 and judge mean 0.6539 are low-precision estimates and the cross-domain 719 contrast (Q7 0.3077 vs Q6 0.1956 vs GPU 0.352) is provisional — r3 landing requires updating the n=3 means and re-judging the cross-domain comparison before the body merges. **(2) 300-r2 contamination.** The 300 tier's third rep is a byte-for-byte copy of r1 (queue-script `cp` bug, `q7-300-r2-dup-rootcause-20261124.md`; independently corroborated by the queue log, which records 300-r2 as rc=1 with wall_s=10115 — a failed/hung run ~2× the duration of the other 300 runs (~4900s) — so the copy is the queue's recovery artifact, not a genuine third rep), so the 300 tier is reported at independent n=2 (cov 0.2692, jt 0.5846); any earlier n=3 figure (cov 0.2564 / jt 0.5615) is superseded and must not be quoted. **(3) Dilution attribution is directional, n=3.** The L5-mediated dilution (with-L5 0.660 vs without-L5 0.707, Δ=−0.047) rests on a with-L5 group of n=3 (719-r1, 719-r2, 300-r3); it is directional evidence, not a conclusion — r3 landing at 719 (if it grows L5) makes n=4 and can harden the attribution, and the causal direction (more nodes thin the verification calls vs deeper nodes are harder to verify) is not separated, so the claim is correlational. **(4) Cross-domain caliber.** The three domains' gold scales differ (GPU 37 / Q6 46 / Q7 52 units; the workshop body's GPU "36" is the older gold revision) and the GPU batch ran pre-clamp (soft cap, overlapping realized counts) while Q6/Q7 ran wall-free at 719, so the cross-domain comparison is a curve-shape comparison on realized node count, not a clean same-budget-across-domains test — the domain-dependence claim ("~400 plateau is not universal") is stated on shape and inherits §4.3's standing pre-clamp confound note. The confound is sharper than a measurement-caliber issue: the GPU batch predates the clamp commit (`47a8543cd`, 11-23 13:44) and ran under a soft cap, whereas the Q7 batch (queue from 11-23 22:55) ran post-clamp under a hard cap, so the tree-growth dynamics themselves differ between the two batches (the hard cap changes per-round expansion behavior) — the cross-domain contrast is therefore a shape comparison across *different growth regimes*, and Q7's nominal budgets are clean realized budgets (measured realized == nominal exactly at every tier, zero overshoot) while GPU's must be read on measured realized counts (`q7-third-domain-integration-20261124.md`, boundary 2, characterized 20:55). Additionally, the T5 parent_ids persistence bug affects this whole batch systematically (719-r1 17/719 empty parent_ids, 300-r1/r2 14/300 each, 50-r1 11/50; dangling=0), but the L5-growth / verified-dilution / redundant-count conclusions do not depend on parent_ids (they use the status field and node counts), so they stand; any parent-link-correctness metric must first exclude empty-parent nodes. **Data provenance:** all structural metrics in this increment are computed from the run JSONs (`q7-719-r{1,2}-*.json`, `q7-300-r1-*.json` — the authoritative, SHA-verified sources that `q7-719-replication-check.py` reads), independently re-verified 2026-11-24 23:05 (r1: 719 nodes, L5=334, ver 0.647; r2: 719 nodes, L5=340, ver 0.679); the `checkpoints/sm-q7-r1` / `sm-q7-r2` directories were overwritten by a concurrent process after the runs finalized (they now hold a different ~57-node run) and must not be used for scoring. Two further caliber footnotes from the 23:05–23:10 re-verification: (a) the 719 wall-free claim is confirmed at the node level — both r1 and r2 realize exactly 719 nodes (no depth-ceiling binding, unlike the Q6 wall-bound 719 tier measured at 197–379 nodes), so Q7's 719 point is a clean full-budget realization; (b) the redundant tier means quoted above (10/14/23/79/354) were computed with the 300-r2 duplicate included in the 300 tier (300: r1=43, r2=43 copy, r3=151 → n=3 mean 79.0); on the independent n=2 caliber (r1+r3) the 300 mean is 97.0, so the sequence is 10/14/23/97/354 — the monotone-redundancy claim holds under either caliber, and the 719-vs-50 contrast (354 vs 10) is unaffected; note the ver_rate L5-split (0.660 vs 0.707) uses the r2-excluded caliber throughout.
